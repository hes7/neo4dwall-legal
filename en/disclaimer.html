1. About

About Neo 4D Wall

Neo 4D Wall is a personalization app that offers mobile devices an advanced wallpaper experience enhanced with high-quality 3D parallax, video, live effects, touch effects, and edge effects.

The app aims to provide an enhanced experience through selected categories, the Editor (wallpaper customization tool), parallax effects, an automatic wallpaper changer, and a content library that is updated when new wallpapers are developed.

All wallpapers are for personal use only.

About the “4D” term: The term “4D” used in the app name and descriptions does not imply a physical fourth dimension. It is used to describe a visual wallpaper experience enhanced with 3D parallax, video, live effects, touch effects, and edge effects.
