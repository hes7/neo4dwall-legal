Support / Destek

Help with purchases, subscriptions, privacy requests, copyright notices, and app operation.

Contact

Email: neo4dwall@gmail.com

Please include the app name, device model, Android version, and a short description. Never email passwords, complete payment card details, or purchase tokens.

Subscriptions and purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Neo 4D Wall. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

For billing problems, include the Google Play order number only when requested through the support conversation.

Türkçe destek

Satın alma, abonelik, gizlilik talebi, telif bildirimi veya uygulama desteği için neo4dwall@gmail.com adresine yazabilirsiniz.

Aboneliği yönetmek veya iptal etmek için Google Play → Ödemeler ve abonelikler → Abonelikler → Neo 4D Wall yolunu kullanın. Uygulamayı kaldırmak aboneliği iptal etmez.
