Content Policy

Last Updated: 19 July 2026

1. Permitted Content

Content offered in the Neo 4D Wall app or used locally by the user is limited to the following types:

  • Wallpapers prepared by the developer
  • Stock or licensed wallpapers
  • Wallpapers created locally by the user through the Editor
  • Public-domain or copyright-free images

2. Prohibited Content

The following types of content are prohibited:

  • Unauthorized copyrighted content
  • Adult-oriented or inappropriate images
  • Harmful, abusive, violent, or illegal material
  • Content featuring brands, companies, or celebrities without the necessary rights

If you believe that any content in our library infringes your intellectual property rights, please notify us at neo4dwall@gmail.com.

3. Copyright Claims

If you believe that your copyrighted work has been used without authorization, please send proof of ownership together with:

  • A link to or screenshot of the infringing content
  • A description of the content claimed to be infringed
  • A statement that the information provided is accurate
  • Proof that you own the copyright
  • The name of the rights holder or authorized representative
  • Contact information
  • A good-faith statement
  • A removal request

Content proven to infringe copyright will be removed promptly.

4. Editor — Responsibility for User Content

Users are fully responsible for content they create with the Editor. The developer does not pre-screen or verify user-created content.

5. Use of App Content

  • Wallpapers may be used only for personal device customization.
  • Resale, redistribution, copying, or uploading to other apps is prohibited.
  • Content may not be used commercially.

6. Google Play Compliance

Neo 4D Wall aims to ensure that app content complies with the Google Play Developer Content Policies. If a suspected violation or valid notice is received, the relevant content is reviewed and removed where necessary.
