Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 19 July 2026

1. Information We Collect

Neo 4D Wall aims to respect user privacy. The app does not require account creation and contains no advertising.

Sensitive/Personal Data We Do Not Collect

Name, telephone number, precise location, contacts, and files are not collected automatically within the app. If a user emails us for support, a bug report, or a copyright request, the email address and message content are processed only to respond to the relevant request.

Technical Information That May Be Collected

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Crash logs through Google Play Console
  • Anonymous usage analytics through Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): viewed screens, session duration, and app open/close events. All data is anonymized and aggregated.
  • Content delivery and access control: wallpaper files are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket and delivered through Cloudflare Worker. Requests for protected content include short-lived security tokens.

✅ Neo 4D Wall contains no advertising and uses no advertising SDK.

2. Editor — User Content

Wallpapers created with the Editor are stored only locally on the user’s device. The app does not upload, transmit, or share this content with a server. The content remains private unless the user expressly chooses to share it externally.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses only the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) — Anonymous usage statistics; Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity — technical attestation to help verify that protected requests come from the genuine app.
  • Cloudflare Worker and private Cloudflare R2 (Cloudflare, Inc.) — Protected delivery of wallpaper content and access control.
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play Developer API (Google LLC) — Pro subscriptions and purchase transactions; server-side verification of purchase tokens before granting Pro entitlement.

✅ Security, App Check, access, and purchase tokens are used only to protect the service and verify Pro entitlement; they are not used for advertising or marketing. No advertising network, tracking SDK, or marketing SDK is used.

5. Children’s Privacy

Neo 4D Wall is not directed to children under 13 years of age. Personal data about children is not knowingly collected. A parent or guardian who becomes aware that a child has provided personal information may contact us at neo4dwall@gmail.com.

6. Data Security

Although the app does not store personal user data, communications with Google Play, Firebase, and Cloudflare Worker use HTTPS. Access tokens for protected content are short-lived.

  • User- and event-level data covered by Firebase Analytics is retained according to the data retention period configured in the Google Analytics property and is automatically deleted by Google at the end of that period. The retention period is managed through the Google Analytics settings.
  • Under Google Play Android Vitals, crash and ANR data may be collected by Google from the devices of users who have enabled sharing of usage and diagnostic data. The developer can access this data in Play Console for the most recent 90-day period and does not separately store it on its own servers. Google’s processing and retention of this data are subject to Google’s applicable terms of service and data retention policies.

7. Data Sharing

Neo 4D Wall does not sell user data to any third party or share it for marketing purposes. Only the technical service providers listed above — Cloudflare Worker and private Cloudflare R2 (Cloudflare, Inc.); Firebase Analytics and Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity (Google LLC); and Google Play Billing and Google Play Developer API (Google LLC) — may process the limited technical, security-attestation, or purchase-verification data required to provide their services.

8. User Rights

Users have the following rights:

  • Stop data collection by uninstalling the app.
  • Clear the cache through App Settings.
  • Cancel their subscription through Google Play.
  • Send privacy-related questions to neo4dwall@gmail.com.

KVKK and GDPR Notice: Under Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), Neo 4D Wall is the data controller. Under Article 11 of the KVKK, users have the right to learn whether their personal data is processed, request information, and request correction or deletion. Users in the European Union may also exercise their rights of access, correction, deletion, and objection under the GDPR. All requests may be submitted to neo4dwall@gmail.com.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is published in the app and/or on the official publication page. Where possible, users may be informed through appropriate methods when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions: neo4dwall@gmail.com
